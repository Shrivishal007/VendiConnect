Link WhatsApp with Chrome

Connects your real WhatsApp account to the VendiConnect bot via Google Chrome and whatsapp-web.js.

Click "Start Chrome WhatsApp Client" below to generate a QR code.
  1. Click Start Chrome WhatsApp Client below.
  2. Open WhatsApp on your mobile phone.
  3. Tap Settings or Menu (⋮) → Linked Devices.
  4. Tap Link a Device and point your camera at this QR code.

Or use the WhatsApp Web Interactive Simulator without linking a phone.
